7. OPERATIONAL RECOMMENDATION & JUSTIFICATION
Recommended Operational Confidence Threshold: 0.25

The empirical sensitivity analysis demonstrates remarkable stability of the Drone Health Score across the entire confidence spectrum. Moving from conf=0.25 to conf=0.40 yields an average absolute score shift of only 0.58 percentage points, with an exceptional Pearson correlation (r = 0.9994) and Spearman rank correlation (rho = 0.9979). 98.8% of images (494/500) remain in the identical health category. Even when escalating to conf=0.75, the mean score shift is only 1.69 percentage points, Pearson r remains 0.9958, and 95.6% of images maintain their exact category. Furthermore, detection confidence across all five classes is exceptionally high (dataset-wide median 0.9315, with Healthy at 0.9344 and Dead at 0.9321). Increasing the threshold to 0.50 or higher discards valid small and chlorotic palm detections without any measurable gain in diagnostic consistency. Therefore, the operational baseline threshold of 0.25 is retained with strong empirical support.

8. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS & FUSION READINESS
- Drone Health Score is an image-level phenotypic proxy; visible RGB cannot detect pre-visual
  physiological stress (e.g. Ganoderma basal stem rot) without multispectral red-edge/NIR bands.
- Replanted blocks (52000_20000 series) exhibit 0% health scores because immature palms are classified
  as 'Small', not 'Healthy'. Future scoring revisions should incorporate a dedicated 'Vigour Score'.
- Block_ID spatial mapping remains BLOCKED until georeferenced orthomosaic boundaries and GIS estate
  shapefiles are acquired. Independent image-level processing maintains scientific integrity.
